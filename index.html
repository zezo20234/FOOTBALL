Mechanics

Matches are turn-based: when you get the ball, play stops and waits for you. Tap a section to open it.

⏱Your turn
  • You have 6 seconds (the bar along the bottom of the pitch). If you run out, the AI plays the move for you.
  • Every teammate shows a pass success %: green is safe, yellow is risky, red is unlikely.
  • You can also tap a teammate on the pitch to pass to them.
  • Buttons fire when you let go. Joystick and buttons work at the same time.
🕹Joystick (left thumb)
  • Passing: point the stick at a teammate. The one closest to that direction is highlighted. Normal passes prefer near players, lob and driven passes prefer far ones.
  • No stick: the game picks the best receiver from positions and your passer's stats.
  • Through ball: with the stick, only teammates ahead of you count. Without it, an attacker is picked for you.
  • Shooting range: inside the box is the best place. From the edge of the box it gets harder, from long range only top shooters can score, and from midfield it almost never goes in. A better shooting stat gives more range, and a strong keeper saves more.
  • Shot aim: push up or down to aim at a corner. A crosshair shows on the goal when you are close enough. Corners are a bit riskier to hit, but much harder for the keeper to stop.
🎮Buttons (right thumb)
  • Pass Ground pass. Depends on your passer vs the nearest defenders, plus distance. Forward passes are a little harder.
  • Shoot Finesse shot, based on shooting and dribbling.
  • Through Ball in behind to a forward. About 14% offside risk in the final third.
  • Chip Chip shot over the keeper. Great close to goal, poor from distance.
  • Dribble Beat the defender: your dribbling vs his defending. Fail and you lose the ball or give away a foul (maybe a card).
  • Tackle (left side, or Q) works while the opponent has the ball, also in online matches (there it must reach him before he plays his move, and you wait 4 seconds between tries). Your nearest player goes in: his defending against the attacker dribbling and pace. A miss can give away a foul or a card, and there is a short wait before you can tackle again.
  • Power (left, hold) makes Pass a driven pass and Shoot a power shot.
  • Auto (left) lets the AI play this move.
  • A goalkeeper with the ball can only pass.
🎯Pass types
  • Ground Tap Pass. Safe and accurate over short and medium range.
  • Driven Hold Pass, or hold Power and press Pass. Travels much further and rewards a good passer. Over-hitting a short ball is punished.
  • Lob Pass + Chip. Goes over defenders and further, but is harder to control.
⚡Charging
  • Hold Pass or Shoot: the first 0.26 s counts as a tap, then the white bar fills over about 1.1 s.
  • Power shots use shooting and physical. Overcharging past about 92% makes them fly wide, and overcharged driven passes are punished too.
🔥5 combos: two buttons at once

Press both together, or within a split second of each other.

  • Pass+ShootCross
    Whips it into the box for a header. Needs teammates in the box. The duel uses physical and shooting vs the defender.
  • Pass+ChipLob pass
    Over the defence and far, but harder to control.
  • Pass+ThroughDriven through ball
    Faster ball with a better success chance and half the offside risk.
  • Pass+DribbleOne-two
    Very safe when short, risky when long. A good one with the receiver in the attacking area makes his next shot a one-on-one finish.
  • Shoot+DribbleSkill shot
    Finesse shot with extra power from your player's dribbling. Aim the corner with the stick.
🏟Team factors
  • Your team rating and chemistry give an edge on almost every success chance.
  • Each red card lowers your team's edge.
  • Player positions are random every match, so no two games start the same.
  • Some AI opponents have bonuses to finishing, passing or goalkeeping.
